## Processing and carriers

Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days, then shipped with DHL, FedEx or UPS depending on your location. You'll get a tracking number by email once the order ships.

## Order statuses

- **Processing:** we're preparing your order. It can still be changed or cancelled.
- **In transit:** the carrier has it. Use the tracking number on the carrier's site for live updates.
- **Delayed:** the carrier reported a delay. We show the updated delivery estimate in your order.
- **Delivered:** the carrier confirmed delivery.

## Delayed or missing deliveries

Delays are usually resolved within 3 business days. If an order is more than 5 business days past its original estimate, or tracking shows delivered but you don't have it, contact support. Our team can open a carrier investigation and, where appropriate, offer a replacement or compensation.
