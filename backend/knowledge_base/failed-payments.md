## Why a payment fails

Common causes are an expired card, insufficient funds, or your bank blocking an international charge. We retry failed payments automatically after 3 and 7 days.

## Paying an overdue invoice

Update your card under **Settings → Billing → Payment method**, then select the overdue invoice and choose **Pay now**. For your security, our support team and chat assistant can't take card details in chat; please never paste your card number into a conversation.

## What happens if an invoice stays unpaid

Your account stays fully active for 14 days after the due date. After that it moves to read-only mode until the balance is paid. No data is deleted.
