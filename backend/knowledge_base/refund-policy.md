## What can be refunded

We refund subscription invoices that have been paid within the last 30 days. Each invoice can be refunded once, in full or in part. Duplicate charges are always refunded in full.

## How long refunds take

Refunds of $50 or less are approved instantly by our support assistant. Larger refunds are reviewed by a member of the billing team, usually within 4 business hours, and you'll be notified of the decision by email.

Once approved, money is returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Your bank may take longer to show it.

## What isn't refundable

Invoices older than 30 days, invoices that haven't been paid, and charges for hardware orders (those follow the returns policy instead).
