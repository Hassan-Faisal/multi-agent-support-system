## Coverage

All Nimbus hardware has a 2-year limited warranty from the delivery date. It covers manufacturing defects and failures under normal use, such as keys that stop registering, dead pixels, or a desk motor that stops working.

## Not covered

Accidental damage, liquid damage, normal wear such as worn keycaps, and damage from unauthorized repairs.

## Making a claim

Contact support with your order number, a description of the fault and, if you can, a photo or short video. A support specialist reviews warranty claims and will arrange a repair or replacement, usually within 2 business days.
