## Export your data

Go to **Settings → Privacy → Export data**. We prepare a ZIP archive of your content and account data and email you a download link within 24 hours. The link expires after 7 days.

## Delete your account

Go to **Settings → Privacy → Delete account**. Cancel any active subscription first. Deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period, during which you can sign in to cancel it. Invoices are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it.

## Privacy requests

For other data protection requests, such as correcting or restricting your data, contact support and a human agent will handle it within 30 days.
