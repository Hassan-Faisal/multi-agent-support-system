## Quick fixes

Work through these in order and test the app after each step:

1. Update Nimbus to the latest version from the App Store, Google Play or nimbus.example/download. Most startup crashes are fixed in a newer release.
2. Force-quit the app and reopen it.
3. Clear the app cache: **Settings → Storage → Clear cache**. On Android you can also do this from the system App info screen. Clearing the cache doesn't delete your synced data.
4. Restart your device.
5. Uninstall and reinstall the app, then sign in again. Unsynced local changes can be lost, so try this last.

## Supported versions

Nimbus supports iOS 17 and later, Android 12 and later, Windows 10 and later, and macOS 13 and later. Older systems may crash on startup.

## Still crashing

Note your app version (**Settings → About**) and device model, then contact support. Our team will escalate the report to engineering with a crash log.
