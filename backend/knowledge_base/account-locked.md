## Why accounts get locked

After 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row, we lock the account for 15 minutes to protect it from password-guessing attacks. You'll see the message "Too many attempts, try again later".

## Unlocking

The lock lifts automatically after 15 minutes; support can't lift it sooner. If you've forgotten your password, use **Forgot password** instead of retrying — resetting your password also clears the lock.

## If you didn't make those attempts

Someone may be trying to access your account. Reset your password, turn on two-factor authentication, and review **Settings → Security → Active sessions**. If you see sessions you don't recognize, sign them out and contact support so we can review the account.
