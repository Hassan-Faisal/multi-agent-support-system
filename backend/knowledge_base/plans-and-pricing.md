## Plans

- **Basic — $9/month:** sync on 2 devices, 10 GB storage, email support.
- **Pro — $29/month:** unlimited devices, 200 GB storage, API access (100 requests/minute), priority support.
- **Enterprise — $499/month:** everything in Pro plus SSO, audit logs, 1,000 API requests/minute and a dedicated account manager.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and you're charged a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current period; you keep your current features until then.

## Billing cycle

Plans renew monthly on the day you subscribed. Invoices are emailed and available under **Settings → Billing → Invoices**.
