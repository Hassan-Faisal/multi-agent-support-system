## Turn on 2FA

Go to **Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication** and scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm. Save the 10 backup codes shown at the end somewhere safe; each works once.

## Lost your phone or authenticator

Sign in with one of your backup codes, then go to Security settings to set up 2FA on your new device.

If you don't have backup codes, support must verify your identity before 2FA can be removed. This is done by a human support agent and usually takes 1 to 2 business days. For your security, AI assistants and chat agents cannot disable 2FA.

## Codes are rejected

Authenticator codes depend on your phone's clock. Turn on automatic date and time in your phone settings and try again with a fresh code.
