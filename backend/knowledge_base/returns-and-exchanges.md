## Return window

You can return hardware within 30 days of delivery for any reason. Items must include their original accessories. Orders that are still processing or in transit can't be returned yet; wait until they're delivered, or ask us to cancel a processing order.

## Starting a return

Ask our support assistant or go to **Orders → Return item**. We email a prepaid shipping label right away. Drop the package at any location of the carrier on the label.

## Refunds for returns

Once the warehouse receives the item, usually 3 to 5 business days after drop-off, we refund the original payment method. You'll get an email when the refund is issued.

## After 30 days

Items past the return window can't be returned, but faulty items are covered by our warranty.
