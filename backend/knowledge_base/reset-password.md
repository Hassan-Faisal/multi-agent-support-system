## Send a reset link

Open the Nimbus app or website and choose **Sign in → Forgot password**. Enter the email address on your account and we'll send a reset link. The link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once.

## The reset email didn't arrive

- Check your spam, junk and promotions folders for a message from no-reply@nimbus.example.
- Make sure you typed the email address you signed up with. If you signed up with Google or Apple, use **Continue with Google/Apple** instead; those accounts don't have a Nimbus password.
- Wait 5 minutes before requesting another link. Requesting several links quickly invalidates the earlier ones.

## Choosing a new password

Passwords must be at least 10 characters. We block passwords that have appeared in known data breaches. Changing your password signs you out of every other device.
